# Objectives

By the end of the lesson the student can:

- talk about summer holidays;
- name objects at the seaside;
- use new summer vocabulary;
- revise [[There is / There are]];
- recognise silent letters.

# Lesson flow

## ☐ 1. Warm-up (5 min)

📖 **PB p.4 Ex.1**
![[Pasted image 20260730220306.png]]
### Goal

Activate holiday vocabulary.

### Ask

- Did you like your summer holidays?
- Where did you go?
- Did you go to the seaside?
- Did you go to the mountains?
- Did you go swimming?
- Did you go fishing?

> [!tip]
> Питати наводящі питання

---

## ☐ 2. Vocabulary presentation (15 min)

📖 **PB pp.4–5 Ex.2A**

Introduce:

- beach
- ![[Pasted image 20260730222635.png]]
- sand
- ![[Pasted image 20260730222705.png]]
- shell
- 
- pebble
- crab
- jellyfish
- seaweed

Then:

- catch a crab
- find a shell
- build a sandcastle
- write a diary
- make friends
- take photos

### Goal

Recognition before production.

> [!tip]
> Use gestures whenever possible (swim, build, write, take photos).

---

## ☐ 3. Vocabulary practice (10 min)

📖 **PB Ex.2A**

Read and repeat.

↓

📖 **PB Ex.2B**

Model:

> There is a jellyfish in the sea.

> There are three boys on the beach.

Student makes more sentences.

### Focus

- [[There is / There are]]
- New vocabulary

---

## ☐ 4. Collocations (5 min)

📖 **PB p.5 Ex.3**

Match verbs and nouns.

Check together.

Examples:

- catch a crab
- find a shell
- build a sandcastle
- write a diary
- take photos
- make friends

---

## ☐ 5. Poem (8 min)

📖 **PB p.5 Ex.4**

1. Listen.
2. Read together.
3. Student reads.

### Focus

- pronunciation
- rhythm
- confidence

> [!tip]
> Don't translate every unknown word.

---

## ☐ 6. Workbook (15 min)

📖 **WB p.4**

### Ex.1

Silent letters.

### Ex.2

[i:] / [e]

### Ex.3

Match transcription and pictures.

---

## ☐ 7. Wrap-up (2–3 min)

Ask:

- What's your favourite new word?
- What can you see at the beach?
- What can you do at the seaside?

Praise.
